# Other halls mentioned

## Philharmonie de Paris (vineyard with balconies)
- Opened on 14 January 2015; designed by Jean Nouvel. [S13] [S14]
- The main hall has 2,400 seats. [S1] [S4]
- Its vineyard concept uses balconies instead of banked terraces. [S4]

## DR Concert Hall (Koncerthuset), Copenhagen (vineyard)
- Opened on 17 January 2009; designed by Jean Nouvel. [S15] [S16]
- The main hall has 1,800 seats. [S1] [S4] [S15]
- Type: vineyard. [S4] [S7]

## Barbican Hall, London (fan-shaped)

- Listed with a 1982 date and 1,943 seats. [S1]

## Tanglewood Music Shed, Massachusetts (fan-shaped)
- A fan-shaped hall that is acoustically successful thanks to reflective upper panels. [S10]

## Royal Albert Hall, London (arena)
