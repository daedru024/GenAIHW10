# Musikverein, Vienna (shoebox example)

**In one sentence:** an 1870 Vienna hall, the standard example of a shoebox hall with a warm, clear sound. [S3] [S4] [S7]

- Opened (inaugurated) on 6 January 1870 and designed by the Danish-born architect Theophil Hansen. [S3]
- The Vienna Philharmonic has performed there since it opened. [S3]
- The Great Hall (Großer Saal), also called the Golden Hall, is about 49 m long, 19 m wide and 18 m high. [S3]
- It has 1,744 seats plus standing room for about 300. [S1] [S3]
- Its lively acoustics came mainly from Hansen's intuition, because no architectural acoustics studies existed to rely on. [S3]
- The narrow shape, thick plaster walls and many diffusing shapes give a warm, rich and very clear sound. [S3]
- Statues along the sides scatter sound and help avoid flutter echoes. [S7]
- Type: shoebox. [S3] [S4] [S7]
