# Weiwuying Concert Hall, Kaohsiung (vineyard example from Taiwan)

**In one sentence:** the concert hall of the National Kaohsiung Center for the Arts, a vineyard-style hall opened in 2018. [S4] [S9]

- Located in Kaohsiung, Taiwan; opened in 2018 with about 2,000 seats in the concert hall. [S1] [S4]
- Architect: Francine Houben; acoustician: Albert Yaying Xu. [S4]
- Described as a vineyard-style concert hall. [S4] [S9]
- Its design kept reverberation time between 2.1 s (mid frequencies) and 2.52 s (low frequencies) and managed noise carefully. [S9]
- Side walls, ceiling height, materials, seating and stage were all designed with architectural acoustics in mind. [S9]

## A note on Taipei
- The National Concert Hall in Taipei is listed with a 1988 date and 2,074 seats. [S1]
- None of the sources in this Wiki describes its shape, so no hall type is assigned to it. [S1]
