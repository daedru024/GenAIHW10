# Vineyard (葡萄園式)

**In one sentence:** the audience sits on terraces around the stage, like the sloping rows of a vineyard. [S4] [S7]

## Shape
- Seating surrounds the stage and rises in rows like vineyard terraces. [S4]
- The stage sits near the centre of the hall, lower than the audience on raised terraces. [S10]
- It can be seen as a musical version of theatre in the round. [S4]
- The type appeared in the early 1960s. [S10]
- The architect of the Berlin Philharmonie reasoned that people naturally gather in circles to listen to music. [S4]
- Also called: "arena or vineyard" in S10. See [conflicts](../conflicts.md) for why this Wiki does not simply equate the two. [S10] [S4]

## Advantages
- Visually interesting. [S7]
- Its irregular pattern helps avoid flutter echoes and focusing. [S7]
- It can hold a great deal of people. [S10]
- Reverberation time can reach values close to the optimum for symphonic music. [S10]
- Walls around the stage help provide early side reflections. [S4]
- Seating blocks can be shaped to reflect sound sideways toward other seats. [S10]
- The uneven sound across seats lets listeners choose seats to taste; S10 argues this is not necessarily a drawback. [S10]

## Disadvantages
- The design is very complicated and expensive. [S7]
- Few lateral reflections arise, because walls are low and far from the instruments. [S10]
- Sound differs a lot by seat: behind the stage, the instrumental balance is almost inverted. [S10]
