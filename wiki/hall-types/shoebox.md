# Shoebox (鞋盒式)

**In one sentence:** a long rectangular room with the stage at one end, famous for strong side reflections. [S4] [S7]

## Shape
- A simple rectangle, typically with some balconies. [S7]
- Stage at one end of a rectangular auditorium. [S4]
- The shoebox became a standard design in the 19th century for architectural rather than acoustic reasons. [S10]

## How it sounds
- Reverberation time of about 2 seconds at mid-frequencies. [S10]
- Listeners feel immersed in sound from every direction, with high clarity and an even sound across seats. [S10]
- Many early lateral reflections arrive between the direct sound and 80 ms, which improves quality. [S10]
- The narrow shape and thick plaster walls of the Musikverein give strong lateral reflections. [S3]

## Trade-offs
- Plain parallel walls can cause flutter echoes unless the room has scattering features. [S7]
- Design by shape alone is no guarantee: a roughly rectangular hall designed in 1962 had poor results. [S10]
