# Horseshoe (馬蹄形)

**In one sentence:** a U-shaped hall with several tiers of boxes, the classic shape of opera houses. [S6] [S10]

## Shape
- La Scala follows a pattern common in late 18th-century Italian theatres: a horseshoe plan with several orders of boxes. [S6]

## How it sounds
- Reverberation time stays close to 1.5 seconds regardless of size, which suits both musical continuity and clear words. [S10]
- The hall and stage form two large coupled acoustic volumes. [S10]
- Balance between the orchestra pit and singers on stage is very good, and clarity is high. [S10]
- S10 calls opera houses the most remarkable case of acoustic stability in Western history. [S10]

## Evidence limit
- Both sources on this type are about opera houses rather than symphony halls. [S6] [S10]

## Example
- Teatro alla Scala, Milan: [la-scala](../halls/la-scala.md). [S6]
