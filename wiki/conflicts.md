# Conflicts and open questions

## Resolved
- **Philharmonie de Paris opening date.** S1 says 2014 and S4 says 2015. It opened on 14 January 2015, so S4 is right and S1 is wrong. [S4] [S13] [S14]
- **DR Concert Hall opening date.** S4 says 2006 and S1 says 2009. It opened on 17 January 2009, so S1 is right and S4 is wrong. [S1] [S15] [S16]

## Unresolved, handled by wording
- **Berlin Philharmonie capacity.** S1 and S4 give 2,440 seats. S8 uses 2,250 in its acoustic-design calculation, and the context of that number is unclear (it may exclude some seats). This Wiki uses 2,440 and does not use S8's figure as a capacity. [S1] [S4] [S8]
- **When did Sabine do his work?** S2 puts his quantification of reverberation in the early 1900s, S10 says late 19th century, and S11 says his experiments began in 1895 and led to his formal definition of RT. These are partly compatible, so the Wiki says the experiments began in 1895 and gives no publication year. [S2] [S10] [S11]
- **Reverberation time targets.** Different ranges (1.7 to 2.3 s, 1.8 to 2.2 s, about 2 s, about 1.5 s) mostly describe different hall types and uses, so they are shown in one table with their sources. [S2] [S7] [S8] [S9] [S10] [S11]
- **Fan-shaped spaciousness.** S7 says the width at the rear seats gives good spaciousness. S10 says fan-shaped halls have little lateral energy and listeners do not feel surrounded. The sources disagree, so both are stated. [S7] [S10]

## Terminology
- **Vineyard, arena or surround?** S4 lists "arena" (Royal Albert Hall) as a different shape from vineyard. S10 treats "arena or vineyard" as one type, with the stage near the centre and terraces around it. The Wiki therefore uses "vineyard" as the main name, mentions arena only as a related term, and does not claim that vineyard is a kind of arena. [S4] [S10]
- **Different lists of "basic" shapes.** S7 lists three shapes, S4 four (with arena), S10 four (with horseshoe). The Wiki follows S10's four. [S4] [S7] [S10]

## Evidence limits
- **Horseshoe** is supported only by sources about opera houses (La Scala and S10's discussion of opera). [S6] [S10]
- **Taipei's National Concert Hall** has no source describing its shape, so it has no hall type here. [S1]
